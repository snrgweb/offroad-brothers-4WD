Measure your starting point

Factory rake, aftermarket accessories and existing suspension wear all influence the final stance. The right kit is vehicle-specific.

Account for towing and payload

A level-looking unloaded truck may sit differently with a trailer or cargo. Discuss how often you tow or carry weight before deciding on height.

Check wheel and tire clearance

Larger tires may require more than a leveling kit. Width, offset, steering clearance and fender geometry matter.

Think about suspension travel

Some setups benefit from compatible shocks or other components. Ask what is appropriate for your exact model and driving needs.

Questions Houston drivers ask

What is the difference between leveling and lifting?

Leveling primarily changes front-to-rear stance; lifting generally aims for additional overall height and may require more extensive suspension changes.

Will a leveling kit affect towing?

It can affect unloaded stance and loaded geometry. Tell the shop about your trailer and normal payload before selecting a setup.

Do I need an alignment?

Alignment needs depend on the vehicle and installation. Ask the installer what inspection and alignment steps are recommended for your truck.
