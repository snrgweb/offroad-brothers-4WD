Start with the way you actually use your vehicle

Offroad Brothers 4WD Store serves Houston-area drivers. Share your exact vehicle details and goals so the team can discuss parts, compatibility and the next steps for your project.

Suspension lift or leveling kit?

A leveling kit typically addresses front-to-rear stance; a full suspension lift can involve additional components and a broader change to suspension geometry.

Tire size and wheel offset

Desired tire dimensions, wheel width and offset influence clearance. A lift height alone cannot guarantee a particular tire will fit without rubbing.

Ride quality and handling

Shocks, springs and related components affect everyday comfort and control. Tell the shop whether your priority is commuting, trail use, towing or a combination.

Installation and follow-up

Ask about alignment, any vehicle-specific calibration requirements and inspection after installation. Exact needs depend on the truck and selected components.

Questions Houston drivers ask

What information should I have ready?

Your vehicle year, make, model, current wheel and tire size, desired tire size, budget and how you use the vehicle.

Will a lift automatically fit larger tires?

Not necessarily. Tire clearance depends on wheel offset, suspension design and vehicle-specific body and steering clearance.

Can I request a quote for my truck?

Yes. Contact the Houston shop with your vehicle details and the lift height or overall look you have in mind.
